# keep privacy policy

**last updated: september 17, 2026**

keep is a minimalist, lightweight browser extension for saving and restoring groups of tabs.

### data collection

keep access the titles and urls of browser tabs that you choose to save. this information is stored locally using your browser's extension storage.

keep does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your saved tab information with the developer or any third party

### data storage

saved tab groups are stored locally in the browser using the extension's storage system. the developer does not have access to this data.

if you remove keep or clear its extension storage, your saved tab groups may be deleted.

### permissions

the storage permission is used to save and retrieve users' saved tab groups using the browser's local extension storage. keep stores the title and url of each tab in a saved group so users can close the extension and later restore their saved tabs.

the tabs permission is used to access the user's currently open tabs so keep can display them in the extension, allow users to select specific tabs to save, and retrieve their titles and urls when creating saved tab groups.

### third party services

keep does not use analytics, advertising, tracking, external scripts, or third-party services other collect user data

### changes to this policy

this privacy policy may be updated if keep's functionality or data practices change. any changes will be reflected on this page.
